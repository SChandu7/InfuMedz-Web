Why we exist

The gap between knowing and doing

Most postgraduate material is written for examinations. It explains what a condition is, lists its features, and stops there. On the ward, that is not the question you are asked — you are asked what to do next, and why.

InfuMedz was built to close that gap. Our lectures and notes are structured around the decisions clinicians actually make: which investigation first, how to read the result, when to escalate, and what the evidence behind it says.

Every course is organised chapter by chapter so you can find a topic in seconds during a busy rotation, rather than scrubbing through a three-hour recording.

Clinically grounded

Content framed around ward decisions and referenced to standard texts such as Braunwald, not exam shortcuts.

Structured, not sprawling

Chapters and lectures are ordered so you can study a topic end to end, or jump straight to the one thing you need.

Yours for life

One payment, no subscription. New lectures added to a course you own cost nothing extra, on the web and in the app.
